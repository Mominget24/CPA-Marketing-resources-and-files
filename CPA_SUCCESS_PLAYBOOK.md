## 0. Executive Summary — read this first

I analyzed all 15 files in this repo: a 12-class Bengali CPA course (plus a duplicate Class 02 and a Pinterest masterclass), and one PDF that is a prior independent safety audit of the course.

**My verdict, in one paragraph:**

> The course teaches the *correct skeleton* of CPA marketing (network → offer → landing page → traffic → tracking → payout) but wraps it around **tactics that get accounts banned and commissions withheld**: fake US personas, fake "Amazon warehouse job" and gift-card/giveaway bait, AI-generated fake addresses, and platform deception. Those tactics produce fast clicks and zero durable income, because networks reverse the leads and platforms kill the accounts. My plan keeps the skeleton, removes the poison, and replaces it with the system actual full-time CPA affiliates run: **one real niche, one network, one offer, one traffic source, ruthless measurement.**

**The three laws this whole plan is built on:**

1. **You are paid for *approved* actions, not clicks.** Every decision optimizes for approved conversions after validation, not dashboard revenue.
2. **Speed comes from focus and measurement, not from tricks.** One funnel measured properly beats ten fake-persona spam accounts. The fastest CPA earners got fast by killing losers in 48 hours and scaling winners in 48 hours.
3. **The asset you keep is the audience, not the commission.** A landing page + email list you own is worth more than any single offer. Offers die weekly; lists compound.

### 1.2 The 5 traps hidden in the course (and why a senior avoids each)

1. **Fake US identity / persona accounts (Classes 4, 6, 8, 9).** Meta and Pinterest run identity confirmation. A Bangladeshi NID cannot confirm a "USA" persona — the account dies exactly when it starts working. Every hour invested = zero.
2. **Fake job / gift card / giveaway bait (Classes 3, 5, 7, Pinterest).** These "convert" because they deceive. Advertisers run fraud filters; these leads get reversed in bulk 7–30 days later. You will watch revenue appear and then vanish, and the network keeps what you already hoped to withdraw. Also: legal exposure under FTC rules (the audit PDF cites this).
3. **Instructor-controlled infrastructure (Class 02, Class no 02).** Students are asked to email their builder-account credentials/details to the instructor "for approval." A professional **owns** their funnel: their own page builder, their own links, nobody else's hands on the machine.
4. **Self-clicking / VPN / bots.** The course itself makes students sign they won't do this — because detection is trivial and the penalty is a permanent ban + frozen balance.
5. **Spending before measuring.** The course pushes paid posts with bonus credits before any conversion data exists. Credits distort judgment — treat them as test tokens with strict kill rules (Section 6), not free money.

## 2. The math you must internalize (5 minutes, saves you months)

```
NET PROFIT = approved payouts − ad spend − fees − reversals − tool costs
```

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Payout** | $ earned per *approved* action (e.g., $1.50–$3.50 for a US email submit) |
| **CR (conversion rate)** | % of landing-page visitors who complete the action. Realistic: 8–25% for clean email-submit funnels |
| **EPC** | earnings per click = payout × CR. This is your compass |
| **Break-even CPC** | max you can pay per click = payout × CR |

**Worked example.** Offer pays $2.00 (US email submit). Your landing converts 15% of visitors.

```
EPC = $2.00 × 0.15 = $0.30 per landing visitor
→ You can pay up to $0.30 per visitor and break even.
→ A traffic source sending 100 visitors for $15 ($0.15/click) → $30 revenue → $15 profit.
```

**Rule of thumb benchmarks (plan against these, then replace with your real data):**

| Metric | Bad | OK | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social post → landing click-through | <0.5% | 1–2% | 3%+ |
| Landing → lead (email submit, US) | <8% | 10–18% | 20%+ |
| Approval rate (leads that stick) | <70% | 80–90% | 95%+ |
| Paid-test ROI | <0% | 0–30% | 30%+ |

If approval rate is under 70%, your traffic or offer is dirty — stop and fix before scaling. This single number is where the course's bait tactics die.

## 3. PHASE 0 — Setup (Day 1, ~3 hours, $0)

Do these exactly as written. This is the boring part that separates people who get paid from people who get banned.

1. **One real identity.** Real name, real Bangladesh address, real permanent Gmail, real phone on every network and platform. Networks pay Bangladesh affiliates legally — faking a US address only buys you a future frozen balance.
2. **Security.** Unique password per account (free password manager), 2FA everywhere, recovery set. **Never share a password, recovery code, or "approval" access with anyone — including a course instructor.** Help via screen-share only, you keep the keyboard.
3. **Payment rails.** Verify a PayPal account in your real name (and/or Payoneer — usually smoother from Bangladesh). Same name on network + PayPal, always. Mismatched names are the #1 reason withdrawals get stuck.
4. **The Tracker.** Copy `templates/tracking_sheet.csv` into Google Sheets. Every link you ever create gets a campaign label. No exceptions. If it's not in the sheet, it didn't happen.
5. **Pick your niche** (choose ONE — criteria below).

### Niche selection — what I'd pick and why

The course pushes "jobs" and "giveaways" because they're easy baits. Seniors pick niches with **real advertisers, repeat offers, and honest angles**:

| Niche | Why | Offer types | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal finance US** (credit scores, budgeting apps, savings) | Highest payouts, evergreen demand | Signups $3–$15, CC submits $20+ | Medium |
| **Health & wellness tips** | Huge Pinterest audience (the course's "Health Viva" instinct was right, the bait was wrong) | Email submits $1.5–$3, trials $20+ | Low-Med |
| **Work-from-home *skills*** (NOT fake jobs: resume tips, remote-skill courses, legit gig platforms) | Captures the same search intent the fake-job bait exploits, legally | Email submits, signups | Low |
| **Apps & tools** (AI tools, utilities) | Installs convert on pure usefulness | CPI $0.5–$3 | Low |

**My pick for your first 90 days: Work-from-home skills + app tools, Pinterest as main channel.** It matches the course's strengths (Pinterest, ChatGPT content), targets US traffic, and needs zero ad spend to start.

## 4. PHASE 1 — Network & first offer (Days 2–5, $0)

### 4.1 Networks (verified against 2026 conditions)

Start with **exactly two**, in this order:

1. **CPAGrip** — the course's main network; keep it. ~2,000 offers, easy approval, content-locker tools, $50 minimum payout, PayPal/Payoneer/wire, weekly → daily as your track record grows [1](https://www.somenbiswas.com/blog/cpagrip-review) [3](https://affmojo.com/network/cpagrip/).
2. **CPAlead** — instant approval, low threshold (~$10–$50), daily payout options once established; great second source of email-submit/install offers [2](https://digitalsolutionsedge.com/blog-cpa-marketing-for-beginners-guide/) [1](https://www.mobidea.com/academy/best-cpa-affiliate-networks/).

**Later (Day 45+, only after you have real numbers):** apply to MaxBounty, MyLead ($20 min payout), Adsterra (smartlinks — the course already covers re-activation in Class 11; keep that lesson).

> Adsterra smartlinks are fine as *supplementary* income on leftover traffic, but a smartlink is not a strategy — you can't optimize what you can't see. Named offers first.

### 4.2 Choosing the first offer — the scorecard

Score each candidate out of 25. **Only run offers scoring 19+.** (Full checklist: `templates/offer_vetting_checklist.md`.)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Action is email submit or app install (no CC/PIN/trial for your first month) | 5 |
| GEO is US/UK/CA/AU and your traffic source can deliver that GEO | 5 |
| Payout ≥ $1.50 | 3 |
| Conversion flow tested by you personally (you walked the whole funnel on your phone) | 4 |
| Affiliate manager confirms your traffic source is allowed **in writing** | 5 |
| Offer has been live >2 weeks with stable cap (not about to die — Class 11's lesson) | 3 |

### 4.3 The 10 questions to send network support (from the audit PDF — send them, save replies)

1. Am I approved for this exact offer and GEO?
2. Is my traffic source (Pinterest organic / short video) allowed for it?
3. Are direct links, landing pages, and sub-IDs allowed? Are cloakers/shorteners forbidden?
4. Is incentivized traffic allowed or banned for this offer?
5. What counts as an approved conversion, and when can it be reversed?
6. Current payout threshold, hold period, payment methods, fees?
7. Disclosure/consent/privacy requirements?
8. Can I use a brand name for my page, and what must stay truthful?
9. Multiple accounts allowed? (Almost always NO — don't ask permission, just don't do it.)
10. Confirm all of the above in writing before I buy any traffic.

## 5. PHASE 2 — Build ONE funnel you own (Days 6–10, $0–$12)

### 5.1 Own your infrastructure (fixing the course's dependency problem)

| Layer | Free option | Paid upgrade (when revenue justifies it) |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Systeme.io free plan / Carrd / network's own lockers | Own domain (~$10/yr) + landing on your domain |
| Email list | Systeme.io / MailerLite free tiers | Same, paid tier at ~1k subs |
| Tracking | Google Sheets (this repo's template) + network stats | Voluum/RedTrack only after $500+/mo revenue |
| Creatives | Canva free + ChatGPT | Canva Pro |

**Never build on a page someone else controls.** This is the single biggest professional habit separating seniors from course students.

### 5.2 Landing page anatomy (the 7 blocks)

The course's *structure* (headline → sub-headline → image → CTA → offer link) is fine. The *content* changes:

1. **Headline** — truthful promise matching the niche ("5 Skills That Actually Get You Remote Data-Entry Work in 2026").
2. **Sub-headline** — what happens when they click.
3. **Hero image** — Canva/ChatGPT-made, no brand logos you don't own (no Amazon/Pepsi/Apple imagery — ever).
4. **3 benefit bullets** — specific, provable.
5. **CTA button** — describes the real action: "Enter your email — get the free checklist."
6. **Affiliate disclosure** (visible, near the CTA): *"This page contains affiliate links. I may earn a commission if you complete an offer, at no cost to you."* — FTC requires this for US audiences [audit PDF ref 3].
7. **Privacy line** — what you collect, what you don't, contact email.

**The pre-frame rule:** whatever the ad/post promises, the landing page must deliver *exactly that*. Bait-and-switch is how reversals are born.

### 5.3 Link discipline

- One tracked network link per traffic experiment; append sub-ID/campaign tags: `pin-health-01`, `yt-short-03`, `classified-test-1`.
- Test the full path on a **phone** (95%+ of your traffic is mobile): click → LP → offer → confirm the pixel/lead fires in your network dashboard (use the network's test/preview mode — never your own real submission).

## 6. PHASE 3 — Traffic engine (Days 10–45, $0)

This is where the plan gets aggressive. Seniors don't "post and pray" — they run a **content system**.

### 6.1 Primary channel: Pinterest (the course's best idea, done right)

Why Pinterest beats Facebook for beginners: it's a **search engine**, not a popularity contest. A pin can send clicks for 6–18 months with zero friends/followers, and Pinterest explicitly allows affiliate links in moderation [audit PDF ref 4].

**Setup (one account, real identity, niche-named):**
- Business account, niche name (e.g., "RemoteSkill Daily" — not "Prize Vault"-style giveaway names).
- Profile link = your landing page. Real description of what the page is.
- 5 boards matching your niche's search phrases (Pinterest search bar autocomplete = free keyword research).

**Daily system (45–60 min/day, non-negotiable):**

| Task | Volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh pins | 2–3/day | 1000×1500px Canva templates; keyword in title + description |
| Pin copy | ChatGPT-drafted, **you edit for truth** | Never claim a result the offer can't deliver |
| Board organization | Weekly | Keyword-named boards only |
| Outbound link | Every pin → your LP (never raw network links pinned directly — moderation + ban risk) | |

**Weeks to expect traction:** 2–6. Pinterest compounds. The course's mistake was expecting instant results and faking location to force US reach; the real lever is **English keywords in titles/descriptions**, which naturally routes you to US search traffic.

### 6.2 Secondary channel: short-video engine (Week 3+)

YouTube Shorts / TikTok faceless, same niche, same landing page:
- 1 video/day, 20–40 seconds: hook (text on screen) → 3 tips → CTA "free checklist in bio".
- Batch-produce: script 7 with ChatGPT on Sunday, record/edit in one sitting.
- One viral short = more traffic in 24h than a month of pins. But pins are the steady floor. Run both.

### 6.3 Channels from the course — ranked honestly

| Course channel | My ruling |
|---|---|
| Pinterest | ✅ Primary (as above) |
| Facebook page + groups | ⚠️ Only a niche page posting genuinely useful content; NO friend-request farming, NO fake US persona, NO group link-dropping (Meta spam policy bans this [audit PDF refs 6–7]) |
| Classified sites (Recycler etc.) | ⚠️ Optional 3-post experiment in Week 4 with leftover credits — treat as a data test, expect little |
| Reddit / Snapchat / iMessage | ❌ Skip. Reddit communities ban affiliate drops; DM spam kills accounts |
| Bluesky/Twitter | ⚠️ Only if you enjoy writing; slow for CPA |
| Medium / Quora articles | ✅ Quietly strong: one genuinely helpful article/week with disclosed links — Medium allows affiliate links with disclosure [audit PDF ref 5] |

### 6.4 The 30-day content calendar (template)

- **Daily:** 2–3 Pinterest pins + tracker check (15 min).
- **Daily from Week 3:** 1 short video.
- **Weekly:** 1 Medium article; refresh 3 underperforming pin designs; 1 email to list (from Week 5).
- **Weekly review (30 min):** fill tracker, compute EPC per source, kill bottom performer, double down on top performer.

## 7. PHASE 4 — First paid test (Days 30–45, ONLY if Phase 3 produced conversions)

**Gate rule:** do not buy traffic until you have ≥10 *approved* organic conversions. You need a known CR before break-even math means anything.

1. **Budget:** $5–$20 maximum, one source (Adsterra push/pop or the network's suggested source for your offer). The audit PDF's $5 test protocol stands: cap it, log it, never chase losses.
2. **Targeting:** US only (match offer GEO exactly — the course's Class 01 warning about USA-vs-UK mismatch is 100% correct).
3. **Creative:** 3 variants, truthful angles, no brand names.
4. **Kill rules (hard stops):**
   - Spend reaches 3× payout with zero conversions → pause, fix LP or creative.
   - Approval rate <70% → stop, talk to AM before continuing.
   - Any flag/warning from network or platform → stop same day.
5. **Scale rule:** ROI ≥ 30% over ≥50 conversions → raise budget 20–30% every 2–3 days. Never double overnight; traffic quality shifts.

---

## 8. PHASE 5 — Weeks 6–12: scale what's real

1. **Weekly AM relationship.** Email your affiliate manager every week with numbers ("300 clicks, 41 leads, 38 approved — do you have a higher-payout sister offer?"). AMs hand their best offers to affiliates who report data. This is the most underused lever in CPA.
2. **Offer laddering.** Same audience, bigger payout: email submit ($2) → signup ($5–$8) → CC submit/trial ($20+) — only once your approval rates prove clean traffic.
3. **GEO expansion.** Clone the winning funnel to UK/CA/AU with localized copy.
4. **Email list monetization.** From Week 5, every LP collects an email (yours, not just the advertiser's). 1 useful email/week, 1 relevant offer/email. A 2,000-subscriber niche list is a permanent income asset [2](https://digitalsolutionsedge.com/blog-cpa-marketing-for-beginners-guide/).
5. **Network upgrade.** With 4–6 weeks of clean numbers, apply to MaxBounty/ClickDealer-tier networks — approval odds jump dramatically once you can state daily volume and approval rates.

## 9. The operating rhythm (print this)

**Daily (60–90 min):**
- [ ] 15 min: update tracker — clicks, leads, approvals, EPC per source
- [ ] 45 min: create + schedule content (pins/video)
- [ ] 5 min: confirm offer still ACTIVE (Class 11's lesson — inactive links earn $0)

**Weekly (60 min):**
- [ ] Compute CR + approval rate per offer/source
- [ ] Kill bottom creative; scale top creative
- [ ] Email affiliate manager with numbers
- [ ] Check reversals column — rising reversals = stop immediately

**Monthly:**
- [ ] Reconcile: approved payouts − spend − fees = actual profit
- [ ] Re-score offers; retire anything <19 on scorecard
- [ ] One new experiment maximum (new source OR new offer — never both at once)

## 10. Red lines — the senior's never-list

Break any of these and you will lose the account, the balance, or both. Non-negotiable:

1. ❌ Fake identity, fake US persona, fake address/DOB, AI-fabricated people
2. ❌ Self-clicks, completing your own offers, VPN/proxy lead generation, bots
3. ❌ Fake jobs, fake giveaways, gift-card bait, invented salaries or prizes
4. ❌ Brand impersonation (Amazon, Pepsi, Apple, Netflix, banks, government)
5. ❌ Buying followers/engagement, mass friend requests, mass DMs, group link-dumping
6. ❌ Multiple accounts on one network/platform; ban-evasion accounts
7. ❌ Sharing passwords or account access with anyone (including instructors)
8. ❌ Cloaking/opaque redirects hiding where a link goes
9. ❌ Undisclosed affiliate links to US audiences (FTC)
10. ❌ Spending beyond your written cap, or chasing a losing test
