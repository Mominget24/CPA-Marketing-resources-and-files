# Offer Vetting Checklist & Affiliate Manager Templates

## A. Offer Scorecard (must score ≥19/25 to run)

Offer: ______________________  Network: __________  Date: ________

| # | Criterion | Pass? | Points |
|---|-----------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Action is email submit or app install (no CC/PIN/trial during first month) | ☐ | /5 |
| 2 | GEO is US/UK/CA/AU AND my traffic source delivers that GEO | ☐ | /5 |
| 3 | Payout ≥ $1.50 per approved action | ☐ | /3 |
| 4 | I personally walked the full funnel on my phone (load speed, form length, mobile UX, no deceptive claims on the advertiser page) | ☐ | /4 |
| 5 | Affiliate manager confirmed my traffic source is allowed, in writing | ☐ | /5 |
| 6 | Offer live >2 weeks, stable cap, still ACTIVE today | ☐ | /3 |

**TOTAL: ____ / 25** — Run only if ≥19. Re-check item 6 weekly (Class 11 rule: inactive offer = zero revenue).

## B. Hard disqualifiers (any ONE = reject, regardless of score)

- Requires or encourages fake user data to convert
- Advertiser page impersonates a brand, employer, government, or prize sponsor
- No clear privacy/consent flow on the advertiser's form
- AM refuses to answer the traffic-source question in writing
- Offer is banned-restricted on my chosen platform (e.g., gift cards on Facebook Ads)

## E. Kill/Scale decision table

| Signal (from tracker, ≥3 days of data) | Decision |
|---|---|
| 0 conversions after 100-150 LP clicks | Change LP headline/design first; if still 0 after another 100 clicks → swap offer |
| Approval rate <70% | STOP. Ask AM why. Do not scale dirty traffic |
| EPC below traffic cost (paid) | Pause ad set, fix targeting/creative |
| ROI ≥30% over ≥50 conversions | Scale budget +20-30% every 2-3 days |
| Reversals rising week over week | Stop offer, demand explanation before any further traffic |
